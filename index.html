Open Files

Desktop apps,
in a browser tab.

Each app is a one-time download of a few dozen megabytes, then runs entirely in your browser. Your files stay on your device and are never uploaded.

Open-source rebuilds by ArtCraft. This website checks for new versions every 15 minutes.
Both daijro and ArtCraft are not affiliated with Adobe, Microsoft, Autodesk, or Avid. These tools were not made by decompiling proprietary binaries.

This site's source: daijro/openplayground.

Latest commits are unstable

These builds come straight from each app's newest code, before it's released. They're untested and highly unrecommended: an app may not load, may crash, or may lose your work.
