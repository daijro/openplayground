to the apps you know.

Rebuilt & patched with Wasm optimizations. Checked for new releasescommits every 15 minutes.

Each card is named after the commercial app it's an open-source alternative to. The projects here aren't made by, affiliated with or endorsed by those companies, and the names are trademarks of their owners.

Each app is a one-time download of a few dozen megabytes, then runs in your browser. Sound libraries load as needed. Files stay on your device unless you choose an app's online sharing.
